Dedicated Developer (Staff Augmentation)

A Flutter developer who works only on your product, as part of your team, reporting to you. Best when you have a team and a roadmap and need skilled hands fast.

  • Monthly or hourly billing
  • Scale up or down as needed
  • You manage day-to-day, we handle HR, infra, and backup
Project-Based (We Build It For You)

Hand us the idea or the spec and we deliver the finished app, end-to-end, on a fixed price and timeline. Best when you want an outcome, not a hire.

  • One fixed price, agreed upfront
  • Full team: PM, designer, developers, QA
  • Weekly demos and a clear launch date
Hybrid

Start project-based to launch, then keep a dedicated developer on for growth and maintenance. Many founders do exactly this.
